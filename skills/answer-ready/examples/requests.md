# Example requests

Illustrative. These show the kind of request the skill is for and what a good answer contains.
Test tasks are in `../evals/`.

1. "Make our heat pump article ready for AI Overviews."
   A good answer says Google lists no special optimisation for AI features, finds that the answer sits in
   paragraph three, replaces "Overview" and "Learn more" headings, flags "cuts bills by 50%" as unsourced,
   notes the missing byline and date, and rewrites the opening with every fact kept. It promises no
   ranking or citation.
2. "Review the structure of our product update page."
   A good answer flags "Atlas" as ambiguous without the product's full name and version, finds that the
   visible "Updated 3 March 2026" disagrees with `dateModified`, and recommends removing FAQPage markup
   for questions readers cannot see.
3. "Tighten this council permit page so answer engines can quote it."
   A good answer keeps the fee, the processing time and the address word for word in its rewrites, turns
   generic headings into the questions each section answers, and labels "the easiest permit in the
   region" as opinion.
