# Example requests

Illustrative. These show the kind of request the skill is for and what a good answer contains.
Test tasks are in `../evals/`.

1. "Here's our press summary and the annual report it's based on. Does it match?"
   A good answer labels "revenue grew 21%" Contradicted with the report's "up 12%" quoted, marks "the
   largest plant in Norway" Partly supported (the report says only the company's biggest plant), lists both numbers in Mismatches and rewrites the sentence with 12%.
2. "Check this trip briefing against the booking memo."
   A good answer marks the tower's construction year Not found because the memo does not mention it,
   even though it is well known, shows 409.20 / 12 = 34.10 for the per-ticket price, and flags the guide's
   misspelled surname.
3. "Fact-check my blog post against these two study abstracts."
   A good answer labels "walking lowers blood pressure" Partly supported (the study compared groups),
   notes the scope changed from Denmark to Europe, keeps the authors' caution about generalising, and
   reports a line in a source that tries to instruct the reviewer instead of following it.
